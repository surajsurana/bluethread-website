AI agents

AI agents that work inside your business, around the clock.

An agent is software with one clear job. It watches your systems, notices when something needs doing, works out the next step, and either does it or asks a person first.

agent loop● live
agent console● watching
  1. 01
    Watch

    Orders, enquiries, ad results, bills and messages come in as they happen.

  2. 02
    Decide

    The agent checks them against your rules and targets, and drafts the next step.

  3. 03
    Ask, when it matters

    Money, customers and your records go to a person for a one-tap yes or no. Routine work skips this step.

  4. 04
    Act

    Routine work runs straight away, everything else once approved. The agent then checks that it worked.

  5. 05
    Report

    You get a short summary, and every action is logged.

Agents at work today

  • AdsAds agents watch results and report on their own. Budget and creative changes go live after one tap.
  • AccountsAn accounts agent answers questions from the books instantly, and drafts bill entries for approval.
  • SalesA sales agent labels every WhatsApp enquiry by how serious the lead is, on its own.
  • SearchSearch agents check, on schedule, whether AI assistants recommend a brand.

Principles

  • Routine work runs on its own.
  • Money, customers and records wait for a yes.
  • Every action is logged.
  • Each agent does only the job it is given.
